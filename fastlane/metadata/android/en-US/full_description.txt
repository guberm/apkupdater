APK Updater finds and installs updates for apps on Android devices from APKMirror, Aptoide, F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, APKPure, GitLab, GitHub, and Google Play.

This edition uses a single TV-style, D-pad-friendly interface on phones, tablets, Android TV, and Google TV.

Features:
* One consistent TV-style interface across supported Android devices.
* Direct APK, APKM, APKS, XAPK, and split-package installation where supported.
* Source buttons that open each provider page.
* Background update checks and notifications.
* Architecture, signature, release-channel, and Android TV compatibility filters.
* Optional root and unattended installation.
* Dark, light, and system themes.
* No ads and no tracking.

Requires Android 6.0 or newer.
